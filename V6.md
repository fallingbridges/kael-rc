# Kael Onboarding — V6 (screen-by-screen)

**Goal: free-trial starts.** Principles:
- The archetype **is** the product — the identity, and what Kael is calibrated to.
- Your archetype = your **gift** and the **pattern costing you**. Kael's promise: keep the gift, lose the cost.
- **Sell in pieces, spread across the funnel** — never dump the whole product on one screen.
- **No auth** anywhere. Name/age/gender up front, as "getting to know you."
- Reveal the archetype **first** (bold), then the read. Thread the real person (J) through everything.
- Every line 5–10 words. No paragraphs, no verbosity.

---

## Act 1 · Promise + getting to know you
1. **Opening** — Hook. "16 ways to love. One is you — and it's why you keep ending up here." CTA: *Find my archetype*.
2. **Social proof** — ★★★★★ 4.8 · two one-line quotes · "Private to you." CTA: *Show me how*. `[sell: trust]`
3. **What brings you here** — Situation chips. Optional: "Someone specific? First name or initial." CTA: *Continue*.
4. **Name** — "What should Kael call you?" Input.
5. **Age** — Quick tap. Auto-advance.
6. **Gender** — Quick tap + Skip. Auto-advance.
7. **Answer honestly** — "No right answers. The messy ones are the truth." CTA: *I'll be honest*.

## Act 2 · The quiz *(the mirror)*
~14 question screens · 4 sections · auto-advance · progress bar · no numbers.
8–21. **Closeness · Attunement · Expression · Tilt** — each: 1 anchor + 2–3 support, mixed formats (two-choice, slider, one multi). Name woven in. One-line live reflection after each anchor. A **breather** between sections ("your type is taking shape").

## Act 3 · Calibrate
22. **Calibration** — Seamless tap; used only to break a near-tie.
23. **Values** — "What matters most? Pick 3."

## Act 4 · Reveal *(coronation)*
24. **Loader** — "Matching you to 1 of 16…" ~3s.
25. **Reveal** — 4 mirrored lines → "You are **The Lighthouse**." Glyph · one essence line · "~1 in 12 love this way." CTA: *Why this is me*. `[sell: identity]`

## Act 5 · Read *(recognition)*
26. **The read** — Gift → shadow (same trait) → "it has a name now" → "right now, with J…". A few pills. CTA: *Continue*. `[sell: it's me]`
27. **Rating** — "Did that land?" Yes / Some / Not really. *Yes* → iOS review.

## Act 6 · The world *(compatibility)*
28. **Who fits a Lighthouse** — "Built for the ___. Undone by the ___." Full 16-map locked. CTA: *Continue*. `[sell: the system + partner seed]`

## Act 7 · Proof *(it works)*
29. **Feel it work** — One free, Lighthouse-specific win on J. "Send this tonight: …" Then: "That's one moment." CTA: *See my plan*. `[sell: efficacy + the chat surface]`

## Act 8 · The plan *(post-read)*
30. **Your plan is ready** — "Maya — your Lighthouse plan." We'll work on: your blind spot · your trigger · your one move. Target: "30 days: distant → reached for." Tiny credibility line ("built on attachment science"). CTA: *See your 30 days*. `[sell: personalization]`
31. **Your first 30 days** — Timeline: Today, bring Kael your moments · Day 3, it learns your pattern · Day 7, first check-in · Day 30, the shift lands. CTA: *Start my plan*. `[sell: chat · memory · check-ins · journey · transformation]`

## Act 9 · The ask
32. **Paywall** — "Your Lighthouse plan." 5 unlocks: full read · the belief · compatibility map · daily coaching · Kael remembers you. "$0 today · 7-day trial · then $9.99/mo." CTA: *Start free trial* · *Not now*.
33. **Winback** — Only after *Not now*. "50% off your first month." CTA: *Claim 50% off* · *Continue free*.

## Act 10 · Begin
34. **Into Kael** — Straight into coaching; first thread picks up J.
35. **Notifications** — After first message. "Check in when your pattern shows up?" → native prompt.

---

**Selling is spread, not dumped:** trust (2) → identity (25) → recognition (26) → the world (28) → it works (29) → your plan (30) → the journey (31) → the ask (32). Each beat sells one facet of Kael; none sells all of it.

**Engine:** V5's instrument (4 axes → 1 of 16, anchor/support, near-tie→calibration, dramatic-safety, confidence→"rare blend," trigger/belief/value/compatibility tags), trimmed to ~14 Qs. Rigor lets the reveal be a confident coronation.
