# Kael Onboarding — V4 (as currently built)

**Kael** is a relationship-intelligence app. Onboarding is a quiz that sorts the user into 1 of **16 Love Archetypes**, reveals it, gives a personalized read, then sells a subscription. **The only funnel goal is starting a free trial.**

- **34 screens.** Phone canvas (393×820), warm editorial aesthetic (Newsreader display titles, Charter serif body, DM Sans for UI). Copy is plain, human, behavioral — no therapy jargon, no poetic vagueness.
- Quiz answer screens **auto-advance** on tap; breather/info screens need a CTA tap. Progress bar, no question numbers.

## Engine (internal — the user never sees the letters)

Four binary axes, each scored by 1 "anchor"-ish two-choice + supporting two-choices/sliders:

| Axis | Side A | Side B |
|---|---|---|
| Closeness | Close (C) | Free (F) |
| Attunement | Attuned (A) | Settled (S) |
| Expression | Expressive (E) | Reserved (R) |
| Tilt | Growth (G) | Harmony (H) |

Code order C-A-E-Tilt → one of 16 archetypes. Two-choice answers score ±1; sliders score graded (−2…+2). Each axis: whichever side has the higher sum wins. **Ties resolve silently** to the lower-key pole (F/S/R/H) — there are no tiebreaker screens, no calibration question, no confidence levels shown.

**16 archetypes:** Harbor, Kindler, Anchor, Cultivator, Devoted, Wildheart, Peacekeeper, Confidant, Kindred, Voyager, Lighthouse, Cartographer, Wanderer, Tempest, Sentinel, Alchemist.

## Screens

### Act 1 — Open in the feeling

**1. Welcome** — *Purpose: congratulate + give hope.* Centered illustration. Title: "You showed up. That is the first move." Sub: "Most people sit with this alone for years. You just chose not to, so let's make sense of how you love." CTA: **Begin**.

**2. What brings you here** — *Purpose: emotional investment + a "doorway" the rest of the flow can reference.* Title: "What brings you here?" Sub: "Pick what's closest. We start there." Tappable chips: I'm spiraling over someone · We keep fighting · They feel distant · I'm getting mixed signals · I'm healing from a breakup · It's good, but I'm scared it won't last · Something else. CTA: **Continue**.

**3. Free-text (conditional)** — only if "Something else" was tapped. "Say it in your words." Single input, max 50 chars. Otherwise skipped.

**4. The promise** — *Purpose: emotional pivot before the quiz.* Type-forward (no illustration). Kicker "NOW, A PROMISE". Title: "There's a *you* that only love brings out." (the word "you" is italic). Sub: "A few honest minutes, and I'll show you how you love, what scares you in it, and what keeps repeating." CTA: **Show me**.

**5. Trust** — *Purpose: reduce privacy fear.* Shield badge, kicker "BEFORE WE START", title "Private, secure, and expert-backed." Sub references attachment theory + relationship science. One grouped panel, two rows (hairline divider): "End-to-end private — No one reads your conversations but you." / "Evidence based — Frameworks used by leading therapists." CTA: **I value my privacy**.

**6. Where are you right now** — *Purpose: light context + warmth.* Title + warm "why we ask" sub ("It changes what helps. Rebuilding after a breakup asks different things than settling into something new."). List: Single and reflecting · Dating someone · In a relationship · Married · It's complicated · Just out of something. Auto-advance on tap.

**7. How it works** — *Purpose: set quiz expectations.* Centered title "Let's find how you love." Sub explains it's not trivia. 3-step icon stepper: "Pick what feels truer (not better)" → "Each one maps how you love" → "Then you meet your archetype." CTA: **Start**.

**Block 3 — closer in:**

### Identity (asked right after the quiz, before the reveal, so the payoff isn't interrupted)

**26. Name** — "Let's make this yours." Sub: "Stays between us, only used to sharpen your read." First-name input. (No account / no auth anywhere in V4.)
**27. Age** — "How old are you?" Warm sub. 5 ranges, auto-advance.
**28. Gender** — "How do you identify?" Warm sub. Woman / Man / Non-binary / Prefer not to say, auto-advance.
**29. Notification** — Bell badge, "Want Kael to check in gently?" Sub: "A quiet nudge when it helps, nothing more." CTA **Yes, check in on me** + quiet **Not now**.

### Act 3 — The mirror

**30. Calibration loader** — "Finding your archetype." Whole-screen % counter (~6–7s), 4 sequential mini-loaders ("Reading your answers / Mapping how you connect / Weighing what you reach for / Putting it into words"), and a rotating 5-star review carousel. Auto-advance.

**31. Reveal** — "YOUR LOVE ARCHETYPE" + warm glyph in a circle + big archetype name + one essence line (e.g. "The fixed point, best loved from a little distance."). CTA Continue.

**32. The read (single page)** — Archetype title up top ("YOUR ARCHETYPE / The Lighthouse"). Then **3 short prose paragraphs** (editorial serif, written per-archetype in plain "holy-shit-that's-me" voice) + a **5-item checklist of one-line pointers** (e.g. "always shows up", "hates being smothered", "goes quiet", "needs alone time"). Closes with *"And honestly? This is just the surface."* CTA: **This is me**. **Nothing is locked or teased — the full read is given.**

### Act 4 — Sell

**33. Calibrated** — Archetype glyph + "CALIBRATED TO YOU" + "Kael, tuned to the [Archetype]." Then **5 concise icon-points** of how Kael helps this type (currently generic placeholder, intended to be per-archetype): catches your pattern the second it starts · helps you say the hard thing in the moment · reads the text you can't read at 2am · keeps what you're working on in view · tuned to how you love, not generic advice. CTA: **See my plan**.

**34. Paywall** — Kicker "You met The [Archetype]" + "Now let's change how you love." 3 feature rows (the full [archetype] read · here when the spiral hits · watch yourself change). Plans: Annual $99.99/yr (7-day free trial) / Monthly $14.99/mo. CTA: **Start 7-day free trial** + quiet **Not now**. (No winback after "Not now".)

## Result & copy approach
Reveal → **one beautiful prose read page** → calibrated sell → paywall. The read is emotionally rich and per-archetype, designed to make the user feel deeply seen. It **gives the full read away** (no locked sections); the calibrated screen + paywall then sell the ongoing coaching.

## Notably absent (vs a full conversion funnel)
- **No social proof / reviews** anywhere.
- **No account / auth step** → no email capture → no abandoned-funnel or price-objection recovery.
- **No values capture; no compatibility, trigger, or belief content.**
- **No rating prompt** before the paywall.
- **No paywall winback** (price-objection users just leave).
- **No locked / teased content** in the result — the read is fully unlocked, so there's no curiosity gap pulling toward the paywall.
- No first-coaching-entry screen (flow ends at the paywall).
