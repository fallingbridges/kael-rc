# Kael Onboarding — V5 (spec)

**Kael** is a relationship-intelligence app. Onboarding is a quiz that sorts the user into 1 of **16 Love Archetypes**, then turns the archetype into personal coaching. **The only funnel goal is starting a free trial.**

Funnel philosophy: *first screen sells the quiz → quiz sells the result → result sells Kael → paywall sells the coaching plan → winback handles price objection → first coaching starts product value.* The full result is computed for everyone; the **free result is valuable but teases locked depth**; paid unlocks depth + coaching.

- **38 screens.** Quiz answer screens auto-advance; breathers need a CTA. Progress bar, no question numbers. No "skip" on quiz questions.
- **Copy rules:** practical relationship language, explain behavior, 5–10 words per sentence (max ~12), pills stay pills, points stay short stacked points. No poetic vagueness. *Good:* "You can mistake low drama for closeness." *Bad:* the long hedged paragraph version of that.

## Engine (internal — letters never shown)

Same four axes as V4 (Closeness Close/Free · Reassurance Attuned/Settled · Expression Expressive/Reserved · Tilt Harmony/Growth), but a more rigorous instrument: each axis has **1 anchor question (max 1.5 pts) + 3 support questions (max 1.0 each)** → max 4.5 per side.

- **Resolve each axis:** higher side wins; strict greater-than, ties use the anchor.
- **Near-tie rule:** if the two sides are within **0.5**, treat the axis as *mixed* and run the resolver: check support-vs-anchor conflict → use anchor if not weak/contradicted → else use the **calibration question** (Screen 25) for that axis → else softer neighbor. (Special: if the Tilt anchor Q13 was answered in the middle (3/4), it's too weak to break a tie — skip it, go to calibration.)
- **Dramatic-archetype safety:** Tempest, Alchemist, Wildheart are "intense" and must be *earned* — their deciding (Tilt) axis must win by **≥0.6**, else fall back to the softer neighbor (Tempest→Wanderer, Alchemist→Sentinel, Wildheart→Devoted). A 0.5–0.6 "dead zone" also falls back.
- **Confidence** per axis = score gap ÷ 4.5 → Mixed / Leaning / Strong / Very strong. Used to soften copy ("You lean Close" vs "Closeness is how you feel safe"). Never shown as a number.
- **Result = the pattern, not the name.** Lead with "Your relationship pattern: [plain description]" then "Closest archetype: [name]" — especially when any axis is mixed or a dramatic fallback fired. Avoids "that's not me" rejection.
- **Triggers / beliefs / values:** every answer adds trigger + belief tags (counted post-quiz); top 4 triggers shown (free 2, paid 2); a belief shows only if tagged ≥2×. Values (picked on Screen 26) personalize the result + first coaching but don't change the archetype.

**16 archetypes:** Harbor, Kindler, Anchor, Cultivator, Devoted, Wildheart, Peacekeeper, Confidant, Kindred, Voyager, Lighthouse, Cartographer, Wanderer, Tempest, Sentinel, Alchemist.

## Screens

### Intro — sell the quiz (4)

**1. Opening** — "You made it here. In a few minutes, you'll discover your love archetype: how you connect, what you need, and where relationships tend to get harder for you." CTA: **Find my archetype**.

**2. Social proof** — ★★★★★ 4.8, two short testimonials (e.g. "My archetype felt so accurate it was uncomfortable, in a good way." — Ashley, 28). CTA: **See what I'll get**. *(Replace with verified reviews before launch.)*

**3. What you'll get** — Three concrete deliverables: 🔍 Your love archetype (how you show up) · 💭 Your blind spot (what you don't realize you do) · 🔗 Your compatibility type (who feels easy/hard and why). CTA: **Start the quiz**.

**4. Answer honestly** — "Pick what feels most like you. Some answers may feel messy. That's the point." CTA: **I'll answer honestly**.

### Quiz — 16 scored questions, story-structured, breather after each section

*Section 1 — How closeness feels (Close vs Free):*
- **5. Q1 (anchor)** "After spending a lot of time together, I usually feel…" → Closer, want more / Good, but ready for my own space.
- **6. Q2** "On a normal day I prefer…" → 4 options from "doing most things together" to "mostly our own things."
- **7. Q3** "When someone shares small updates, I…" → Like feeling included / Don't need frequent updates.
- **8. Breather 1** — "There is no better answer here. Some feel safer with closeness, some with room." CTA Keep going.
- **9. Q4 (Likert 1–6)** "When someone becomes a big part of my routine…" strongly want that → start feeling crowded.

*Section 2 — What distance does to you (Attuned vs Settled):*
- **10. Q5 (anchor)** "When someone goes quiet, my first instinct…" → I notice and wonder what changed / I trust we're okay. (Optional affirming popup either way.)
- **11. Q6** "After a fight that felt resolved, I…" → 6 options from "move on, it's done" to "can't settle until I know we're okay."
- **12. Q7 (Likert)** "A small change in tone/texting can stay in my head." disagree→agree.
- **13. Breather 2** — "Your archetype is taking shape" (closeness, distance, expression, tension). CTA Keep going.
- **14. Q8** "When things feel distant for no clear reason…" → Assume it'll pass / Can't relax until I know.

*Section 3 — How you show love (Expressive vs Reserved):*
- **15. Q9 (anchor)** "When I feel love strongly…" → Let it show / Keep it quiet, but act on it.
- **16. Q10** "If someone needs to know where they stand…" → Tell them directly / Hope my actions make it clear.
- **17. Q11 (Likert)** "If I care, they can usually tell from my actions." disagree→agree.
- **18. Breather 3** — "Some say love out loud. Some prove it quietly." CTA Keep going.
- **19. Q12** "When I need to say something vulnerable…" → Say it imperfectly / Need time first.

*Section 4 — What happens under tension (Harmony vs Growth):*
- **20. Q13 (anchor, Likert)** "When the truth might disturb the peace…" protect the peace → push for the real conversation.
- **21. Q14** "After a hard conversation I want most…" → To feel warm and close again / To understand what it showed us.
- **22. Q15** "When things are calm but not deep, I feel…" → Okay, calm matters more / Like something's missing.
- **23. Breather 4** — "The last question looks beneath the surface… what happens when you care and things feel real." CTA Finish my archetype.
- **24. Q16** "When something small keeps bothering me…" → Let it go / Bring it up / Make things feel good again / Notice but wait.

### Calibration + values (2)

**25. Calibration** — *Always renders, auto-advances; answer only used to break a near-tie.* "Which feels most like you?" → A: closeness, distance affects me fast · B: care deeply but need space · C: show up steadily, don't say much · D: want honesty even when uncomfortable. (A=Close+Attuned, B=Free, C=Reserved+Settled, D=Growth+Expressive, applied only to genuinely tied axes.)

**26. Values** — "What matters most? Pick 3." Consistency · Closeness · Emotional safety · Honesty · Independence · Being understood · Growth · Freedom. CTA appears after 3 chosen. (Personalizes result + coaching; doesn't change archetype. Stored in tap order.)

**27. Quiz complete** — "That's everything we need. You showed Kael how you move toward love, protect yourself, express care, and handle tension." CTA: **See my archetype**.

### Account + profile (4) — *result is gated behind this*

**28. Auth** — "Your archetype is ready. Save your result so Kael can personalize what comes next." Continue with Apple / Google / Email. Small print: result saved privately. **(This gates the result behind signup.)**

**29. Name** — pulled from Apple/Google if available, else "What should Kael call you?" first-name input.

**30. Gender** — Man / Woman / Non-binary / Prefer not to say (+ Skip). "Helps personalize; doesn't define your result."

**31. Loader** — 3–4s animated: "Reading your answers… Finding your archetype… Mapping your blind spot… Your result is ready." Auto-advance.

### Free result (1 skimmable card stack)

**32. Free result** — A scrollable stack of cards; each section has 4 insights, **free shows 2 and locks 2** (e.g. "2 more blind spots found"). Cards: ① Relationship pattern + closest archetype ("Steady love with space → The Lighthouse") · ② What this means · ③ How this shows up · ④ Where it gets hard (blind spot) · ⑤ What you need · ⑥ What activates you (trigger pills) · ⑦ Compatibility (clicks with / may clash with) · ⑧ What Kael helps with. CTA: **See my coaching plan**.

### Sell (4)

**33. Rating prompt** — "Did your archetype feel useful?" Yes, accurate / Some parts / Not really. *Yes* fires the native iOS review prompt; all options then continue. (Captures emotion + acts as a micro-commitment right before the paywall.)

**34. Paywall** — Short, archetype-specific. Example (Lighthouse): "The Lighthouse — Your calm has a cost. Kael helps you see when steadiness becomes distance." ≤5 unlock bullets (full breakdown · the belief driving the pattern · trigger + compatibility map · daily coaching · memory over time). "$0 today, then $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr." CTA: **Start free trial** + secondary **Not now**.

**35. Winback** — *Only if "Not now" tapped.* "Try Kael for less — 50% off your first month. The real work starts when you use your archetype in real moments. Was $9.99 → $4.99 first month." CTA: **Claim 50% off** + **Continue free**.

**36. First coaching entry** — *Product value starts here (full mode if subscribed/winback; limited mode if skipped).* "What's been on your mind lately? Bring Kael anything." Input + prompt chips (I keep overthinking · We had a fight · I feel distant · I don't know what I want · Someone is confusing me). CTA: **Talk to Kael**.

**37. Notification prompt** — *After the first coaching message, never before the paywall.* "Want Kael to check in when it matters? Gentle check-ins when your pattern shows up." CTA **Turn on check-ins** (fires native permission) + **Not now**.

**38. Partner invite** — *Deferred — shown later inside the app, not in onboarding.* Send your partner the same quiz; Kael maps both archetypes.

## Free vs paid
- **Free:** archetype name + tagline + what-it-means, 2 of each (love-style points, blind spots, needs, trigger pills, compatibility points, Kael-help points), and a limited coaching entry.
- **Paid:** full result, all locked insights, attachment flavor, core need, belief + truth, trigger map, values, communication + conflict style, full compatibility map, daily coaching, memory over time.

## Result content per archetype (spec'd for all 16)
Each archetype has: archetype card (name + tagline + one line), What this means / How this shows up / Where it gets hard / What you need / What activates you / Compatibility / What Kael helps with (4 short points each), plus a paid "deeper result" (attachment flavor, core need, belief→truth, protective move, communication style, conflict style, coaching focus) and a one-line paywall hook.

## CTA routing (important)
Free-result CTA → **Rating prompt** (not straight to paywall) → **Paywall**. *Start trial* → first coaching (full). *Not now* → **Winback**. Winback *claim* → first coaching (full); *continue free* → first coaching (limited). Notification prompt comes after the first coaching message.

## Notably present (vs V4)
Social proof up front · explicit "what you'll get" · values capture · account/auth (email capture) · skimmable card-stack result with **locked/teased depth on every section** · compatibility + trigger + belief systems · pattern-first (not name-first) display · rating prompt before paywall · short archetype-specific paywall · **winback offer** · first-coaching-entry to start product value.

## Notably heavier (watch-outs)
16 scored questions + calibration + values + auth + name + gender + loader before the result (~longer, more drop-off points) · the **auth gate sits before the result** (sunk-cost + email capture upside, friction downside — only works well as one-tap Apple/Google).
